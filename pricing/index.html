Pricing questions

What's the difference between a drop-in and the intro offer?

A single intro class is €20 — perfect for a first visit. A drop-in is one class at €32 (one credit, valid a week). The intro offer is three classes for €55 (valid two weeks), the best-value way to get started.

Do class packs expire?

Monthly packs (4, 8 or 12 classes) are valid for one month. Flexi packs (10 or 20 classes) are valid for three months from purchase. Credits are not extendable and unused classes don't roll over.

Can I share a pack with a friend?

Packs are personal to you. A friend can start with a €20 intro class or a €32 drop-in.

What happens if I need to cancel a booking?

Cancel at least 12 hours before class and your credit is returned automatically. See our policies page for the details.

Anything else? Message us — we usually reply within half an hour.
